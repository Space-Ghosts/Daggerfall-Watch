What these numbers mean

Visitors are estimated from a daily keyed hash of network address and browser user-agent. No raw IP address or user-agent is saved in the metrics database. Shared networks can merge visitors; VPNs, browser updates, and changing addresses can split them. Daily identifiers rotate, so daily counts must not be added together as “unique people this month.”

There are no analytics cookies, cross-site trackers, account names, search terms, or purchase records. Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and the site’s metrics opt-out are respected. Recognized bots are excluded, but counts are approximate and can still be manipulated. Thirty days of daily records are retained.
