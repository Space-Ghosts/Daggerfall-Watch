What these rankings mean

This directory discovers guilds through public town-seat standings. It cannot list every inactive or non-territorial guild. Full coverage means all confirmed seats in the server’s registry were read for the current game week—not every guild in the game.

The scanner checks seats one at a time and refreshes again about an hour after finishing. Snapshots can therefore differ in age. Failed or disputed seats are identified in the coverage notice. A website refresh reads our saved snapshot; it does not start a game-server scan.

Contributors are accounts that earned influence at a particular seat. They are not a guild’s total membership and cannot be added across seats to count unique people. Member counts, rosters, treasuries and internal guild notices are not collected.

Game standings include legacy, tribute, and territorial modifiers. These are observed standings, not a measure of raw activity or an official global leaderboard.
