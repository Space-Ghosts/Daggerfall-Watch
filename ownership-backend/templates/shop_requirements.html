Before adding your shop

  1. Set the house’s access to Public in game.
  2. Place a shopkeeper/vendor inside the house.
  3. Put at least one item up for sale with that shopkeeper. The listing must be active and belong to your current game account.

Recently received a house? Create a listing from your own account; the previous owner’s listings do not count. An empty shopkeeper will not appear.

Search using your in-game account name, not your character name, and choose the house’s region. Then open your shop, refresh its stock if needed, and complete the listing verification. Each shop is verified separately.
